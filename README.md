# Logan Hallee

CSO and Founder of [Synthyra](https://synthyra.com/), a Public Benefit LLC building computational protein science systems.
Research Fellow at the University of Delaware. I completed my PhD in Bioinformatics Data Science in the [Gleghorn Lab](https://www.gleghornlab.com/).
I work on protein language models, protein-protein interaction prediction, generative models for protein design, and open-source pLM tooling.
I also write [Minds and Molecules](https://medium.com/minds-and-molecules), a blog on science, computation, and philosophy.
